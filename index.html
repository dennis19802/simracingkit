SimRacingKit

VEHICLE PHYSICS FOR UNITY

Realistic, modular vehicle physics for Unity 6 (URP). Pacejka tires, a complete drivetrain, driving assists and steering wheel force feedback – tune every physical value in the Inspector.

Unity Asset Store Watch videos

Simulation-grade physics, modular by design

Every vehicle is built from independent, preset-driven modules. Drive it as it is, or open the Inspector and tune tires, engine, drivetrain, suspension, brakes and aerodynamics.

T

Pacejka tires

Magic Formula with combined slip, load sensitivity and camber thrust. Three-zone temperature, pressure and wear.

E

Modular engine

Torque curves, turbo and supercharger, ECU with rev limiter and launch control, fuel and thermal model, hybrid and electric drive.

D

Full drivetrain

Clutch with heat and wear, torque converter, manual, sequential, automatic, DCT and CVT gearboxes, open, LSD, Torsen and active differentials.

S

Suspension & brakes

Bump stops, dynamic camber, anti-roll bars. Brakes with disc temperature, fade and pad compounds.

A

Aero & assists

Downforce, DRS and ground effect. ABS, TCS, ESC, engine drag control, launch control and steering assist with assist levels.

W

Wheel & FFB

Keyboard, gamepad and steering wheel via the Input System, force feedback for DirectInput wheels on Windows.
