FAQ

Do I need the Vehicle System to use an add-on?

Yes. All add-ons are built on top of the Vehicle System core. The AI Drivers additionally need the Racing System, because the AI drives on its generated racing line.

What is a "seat"?

One license per developer who works with SimRacingKit in the Unity Editor. Players of your finished game need no license – you can ship unlimited games built with it.

Can I upgrade to the Complete Bundle later?

Yes. Contact us with your order number and we will credit what you already paid.

Which Unity version is required?

Unity 6000.3 (Unity 6.3 LTS) or newer, Universal Render Pipeline and the Input System package.
